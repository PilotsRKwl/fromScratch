**Your bank is full. Your adventure starts at zero.**

Friends Beta 10.8.1 gives you more reasons to rebuild.

- **Reclaim more equipment**, including corrupted crystal weapons, Emberlight, crystal armour, imbued rings and Dizana's Quiver.
- **Unlock affordable skilling outfits.** Carpenter, Pyromancer, Raiments of the Eye, Smiths' Uniform and Zealot sets cost 2M virtual GP each.
- **Work toward more utility and storage buybacks**, from the Abyssal Lantern to your favourite gathering containers. Existing contents remain legacy possessions.
- **Find your next goal faster** with alphabetized Utility & Storage, Weapons, Armour and Skilling Outfits.
- **Enjoy improved Virtual Bank searching** with a fix for the search box closing as it opens, while keeping native bank tabs and your challenge view.

All buyback prices are fixed gameplay costs paid with virtual GP. Your real coins and items stay yours.

**Download FromScratch-Beta-Setup.exe below.** Windows 64-bit. Java is included. Updating preserves existing local progression; export a private backup before updating.
